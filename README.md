## AI Governance, Adversarial Assurance, and Security Risk

I build evidence-centered governance and assurance methods for high-consequence
AI, cybersecurity, security, and investigative decisions. My work combines Red
Team methodology, multi-model evaluation, control frameworks, legal analysis,
and accountable human judgment.

My professional background spans military intelligence, adversarial Red Team
operations, complex investigations, corporate security, crisis operations,
enterprise risk, compliance, and international business. I am currently
completing a Master of Legal Studies in Cybersecurity Law & Policy at Texas A&M
University School of Law.
